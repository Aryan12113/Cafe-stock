Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Cafe Stock is a web app that helps cafes track ingredients, menu items, sales and purchases. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign up with email, we store your email address and a securely encrypted password.
  • Cafe data you enter: your cafe name, ingredients, stock levels, prices, recipes, sales and purchases.

How we use it

  • To log you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show you your own cafe's data and the reports built from it.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, or share your data with anyone else. Each cafe can see only its own data.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and login provider. Google sign-in is provided by Google. These services process data only to run the app.

Google user data

Cafe Stock uses Google sign-in only to confirm who you are. We use your name, email address and profile picture for your account, and nothing else. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all of your cafe's data at any time by emailing 312studio.builds@gmail.com. We will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? Email 312studio.builds@gmail.com.
